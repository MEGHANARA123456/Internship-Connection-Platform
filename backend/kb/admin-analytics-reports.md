## Review platform analytics and reports
Open **Platform Admin Dashboard** at `/admin` for platform-wide metrics, overall operational statistics, and administrator dashboard figures. Open Reports (shown as Disputes in navigation) to review submitted reports and change a report from Open to Investigating, then to Resolved when the review is complete. Document the resolution when closing a report; there is no separate dispute workflow. Use **Audit Logs** at `/admin/audit-logs` to inspect recent administrator actions and understand which changes were recorded. Aggregate dashboard figures describe recorded platform activity and should not be presented as forecasts.

## Platform audit records
Platform audit records are listed on **Audit Logs** at `/admin/audit-logs`, which presents the action, actor, target, and timestamp for each recorded admin action. This is the place to inspect recent administrative activity, moderation history, and platform operations without exposing private student data.
