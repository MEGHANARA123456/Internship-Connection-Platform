## Find people who applied
To review applicants, open Applicants at `/company/applicants` and choose a posting.
1. Open Applicants, or open a posting's Applicants page at `/company/jobs/:id/applicants`.
2. Use Filter by internship, candidate name, skills, education, or verified skill to narrow the list.
3. Review the application status, date, profile, skills, badges, and uploaded resume on the candidate card.
4. Use the card's Actions menu for an available hiring action.

The list contains applications to postings owned by your company, not other companies' applicants. Use Hiring Pipeline at `/company/pipeline` when you want to compare applicants grouped by stage, or Shortlisted at `/company/shortlisted` to focus on shortlisted candidates.

## Open an applicant profile and resume
To review a candidate's profile and uploaded resume, use the candidate card in Applicants.
1. Open Applicants at `/company/applicants` and choose the relevant posting.
2. Select the candidate's card and choose the profile or resume action.
3. Review only applicants to postings owned by your company.

## Shortlist a candidate
To shortlist a candidate, use the allowed action on an applicant in Under Review.
1. Open Applicants or Hiring Pipeline and select the candidate.
2. Confirm the application is at Under Review.
3. Choose Shortlist from Actions; the platform updates the stage and notifies the student.
