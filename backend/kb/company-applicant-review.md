## Find people who applied
Open Applicants at `/company/applicants` to review applications to your company's internships. To view applications for one posting, open that internship's Applicants page at `/company/jobs/:id/applicants`. On Applicants, use Filter by internship to choose the posting. Search by candidate name, and use Filter by skills, Filter by education, and Filter by verified skill to narrow the results. Applicant cards show the student's name, internship, education, application status, application date, skills, and any verified skill badges. Use Actions on a card to open the candidate's profile and uploaded resume, review the application, or continue with an allowed hiring action.

The list contains applications to postings owned by your company, not other companies' applicants. Use Hiring Pipeline at `/company/pipeline` when you want to compare applicants grouped by stage, or Shortlisted at `/company/shortlisted` to focus on shortlisted candidates.
