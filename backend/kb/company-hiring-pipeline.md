## Open Hiring Pipeline
To move a candidate through hiring, Open **Hiring Pipeline** at `/company/pipeline` to see applicants grouped by their application stage. The current stage determines the allowed next stage; use the candidate's **Actions** menu to choose an available action.
1. Open Hiring Pipeline to see applicants grouped by stage, or open Applicants or Shortlisted.
2. Open a candidate card and choose an action available for the current stage, or drag it to a permitted stage.
3. Follow the allowed transitions; candidates cannot skip a stage or leave a terminal stage.

## Meaning of each stage
The stages mean:
- Applied: student submits an application.
- Under Review: company reviews the application.
- Shortlisted: selected for further consideration.
- Interview Scheduled: interview arranged.
- Selected: offer extended.
- Accepted: student accepts the offer.
- Rejected: company declines; final stage.
- Withdrawn: student withdraws; final stage.

## Which moves are allowed
The allowed next stage depends on the current stage; the Actions menu shows available moves.
1. Applied can move to Under Review.
2. Under Review can move to Shortlisted or Rejected.
3. Shortlisted can move to Interview Scheduled or Rejected.
4. Interview Scheduled can move to Selected or Rejected; Selected can move to Accepted.
5. Students can withdraw from Applied or Under Review. Accepted, Rejected, and Withdrawn are final; students receive relevant updates.

## Decline or reject an applicant after review
To decline or reject an applicant after reviewing an application, open **Hiring Pipeline** or **Applicants**, select the candidate's **Actions** menu, choose **Reject**, and confirm **Reject applicant**. Rejected is a final stage and the student is notified.
