## Manage platform accounts
Open Platform Users at `/admin/users` to search for an account and review its status. To permanently delete a user, confirm the target account, choose Delete user, enter the appropriate reason, and type the exact email to confirm; this cannot be undone. These controls are restricted to administrators.

## Company directory and user list
The company directory is available from the admin navigation under Company Directory or Users Management. Use the directory to review business profiles, account status, and verification state without exposing confidential student details.
