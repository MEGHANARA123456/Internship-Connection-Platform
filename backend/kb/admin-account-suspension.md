## Suspend an account
To suspend an account, open **Platform Users** at `/admin/users`, find the account, and select the **Suspend** button on its row. Confirm you have selected the intended account before changing its access. The row then shows the status as **SUSPENDED** and the button changes to **Reactivate**.

## Reactivate an account
Open Platform Users, find the suspended account, and select **Reactivate** on its row to restore its active status.
