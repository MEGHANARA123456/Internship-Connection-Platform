## Find internships by city, work schedule, and field
Open **Opportunities** and enter words from an internship title or company name in search. Use **Location** for a city or remote location, **Filter by work mode** to select **Remote**, **Hybrid**, or **On-site**, and **Industry** and **Skills** to narrow by field or required skills. Combine filters to focus the list, or select **Clear filters** to see broader results. Open a card for full requirements, eligibility, dates, and application details. Save a role to revisit it later; saving does not submit an application.

## Filter internship results for remote work
On Opportunities, enter a city in **Location** or choose **Remote** in **Filter by work mode** to limit internship results to remote work and remote opportunities. Use **Industry** to select a professional field and **Skills** to match required skills. These filters can be combined; use **Clear filters** to reset them.
