## Privacy preferences and data rights
Open **Privacy & Data Security Center** at `/privacy-center`. Select **Cookie Preferences** to open the cookie choices, update the available preferences, then select **Save Preferences**. Follow the account data controls to request access, export, or deletion where provided. The chatbot conversation is kept in browser memory only and is cleared when you sign out or switch accounts; it is not a permanent chat-history feature.
