## Troubleshoot a missing page or action
Confirm that you are signed in with the correct account and role; pages and actions are available only to roles with permission. If company settings or profile are unavailable, check your company account and open Company Profile. Refresh, check your connection, and retry. For a missing verification status or email, check spam and confirm the registered address. Contact support with the page and brief details, never a password or one-time code.

## Renew an expired email verification link
If an account confirmation or email verification link expired or is no longer valid, open **Email Verification** at `/verify`. Select **Send a new verification email**, enter the registered address, and choose **Resend email**. The new link is valid for 24 hours; older links no longer work.
