## Student, company, and administrator account options
The available account types are Student, Company (recruiter), and Administrator. Student accounts manage profiles, resumes, saved internships, applications, interviews, messages, and notifications. Company or recruiter accounts manage organization profiles, postings, applicants, interviews, and recruiting analytics. Administrator accounts manage posting moderation, company verification, user accounts, reports, analytics, and audit records. Each role sees only its permitted pages and data.
