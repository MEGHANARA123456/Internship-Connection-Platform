## Verified badges and expiry
A passing assessment earns a verified badge that expires 12 months after it is earned.
1. Open the badge area on your Profile to check whether the badge is valid and see its expiry date.
2. The assessment result and PDF report also show the expiry date.
3. A verified badge is not a job offer or a guarantee of hiring; companies may see relevant badges when the application workflow permits it.
