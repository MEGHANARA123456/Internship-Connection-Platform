## Warning signs of a suspicious or fake internship
To spot a suspicious or fake internship, be cautious if someone asks for money to apply, promises guaranteed earnings without a clear role, pressures you to act immediately, or requests passwords, verification codes, banking details, or unnecessary identity documents. Check that the company and role details are consistent and that the conversation matches the posting.

## Protect yourself and report
Do not pay application or placement fees. Avoid opening unexpected links or files, and use platform messaging where possible. If something seems suspicious, stop sharing information and report the posting or interaction through the relevant InternSphere page. Reporting lets the platform team review it; it does not guarantee a particular outcome.
