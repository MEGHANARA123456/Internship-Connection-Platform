## Review moderation reports
To review a report submitted about platform content or conduct, open **Dispute & Flagged Reports** at `/admin/reports`. This is separate from **Jobs Queue**, which is for reviewing pending internship postings.
1. For an Open report, select **Investigate**.
2. For an Investigating report, select **Resolve Dispute**.
3. Enter resolution notes in **Resolution Notes**, then select **Mark as Resolved**.
