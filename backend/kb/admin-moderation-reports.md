## Review moderation reports
To review a complaint or report about a flagged internship, listing, user, or platform conduct, open **Dispute & Flagged Reports** at `/admin/reports`. This is separate from **Jobs Queue**, which is for reviewing pending internship postings.
1. For an Open report, select **Investigate**.
2. For an Investigating report, select **Resolve Dispute**.
3. Enter resolution notes in **Resolution Notes**, then select **Mark as Resolved**.

## Complete and close an investigation
To finish or complete an investigation and record its outcome, open the report in **Dispute & Flagged Reports**, choose **Investigate**, and review the submitted reason. When the investigation is complete, select **Resolve Dispute**, document the outcome in **Resolution Notes**, then choose **Mark as Resolved**. The report's statuses are Open, Investigating, and Resolved.

## Handle a reported or flagged posting
For a complaint about a reported or flagged posting or internship, open the report in **Dispute & Flagged Reports** at `/admin/reports`. Choose **Investigate** to review the submitted reason. When the investigation is finished, select **Resolve Dispute**, document the resolution in **Resolution Notes**, and choose **Mark as Resolved**.
