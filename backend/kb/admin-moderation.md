## Review pending internship submissions
New internship posts submitted by companies and pending internship submissions awaiting approval are reviewed in the administrator **Jobs Queue** at `/admin/moderation`.
1. Open Jobs Queue and select a pending posting.
2. Check its description, eligibility, location, skills, and deadline.
3. Choose Approve for a compliant posting or reject it with a clear reason.
4. The action is recorded in the audit log and the company receives the relevant update.

## Approve or reject a posting
To approve or reject a submitted posting, review it in Jobs Queue and choose Approve or Reject with a clear reason.

## Review pending postings
To review pending postings, use Jobs Queue at `/admin/moderation`.
1. Open Jobs Queue and filter for pending items.
2. Select a posting and inspect its details.
3. Choose Approve or Reject; the queue contains posting information, not private student application data.

## Review audit events
To review audit logs:
1. Open Audit Logs from the administrator dashboard.
2. Review recorded administrative actions by actor, action, target, and time.
Use this history for operational review; avoid copying private user details into chat or public reports.

## Where moderation actions appear
To see a moderation decision later, review its record in Audit Logs.
1. Open Audit Logs from the administrator dashboard.
2. Find the event by actor, affected posting, time, or decision.
