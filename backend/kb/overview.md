## What InternSphere does
InternSphere connects students looking for internships with companies recruiting interns. Visitors can browse public internship opportunities before creating a profile; an account is needed for student or company workflows. Students can create profiles, discover and save internships, apply, track application updates, and coordinate interviews. Companies can publish opportunities, review applicants, manage hiring pipelines, and measure recruiting activity. Administrators moderate postings, verify companies, manage accounts, and review platform activity.
